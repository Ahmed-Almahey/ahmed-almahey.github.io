###  Project Overview

This project focuses on building **analytical data cubes** using **SQL Server Analysis Services (SSAS)**.  
The aim was to design a structure that helps businesses **analyze and visualize sales performance** from multiple perspectives — by product, customer, channel, and time.

Through this project, I explored how data modeling and multidimensional analysis can turn raw data into meaningful insights for decision-makers.  
By defining dimensions, measures, KPIs, and calculated fields, I was able to make the data tell a story — not just show numbers.

---

###  Business Value

In real-world business environments, raw sales data is often scattered across transactional databases.  
SSAS allows transforming this data into **OLAP cubes** — structured models that make it easy for managers and analysts to explore performance trends, track KPIs, and compare results across time periods, products, and markets.

For example, in this project:
- The **Product Cube** helps analyze product quantity trends across different years.  
- The **Prod_Cust Cube** links customer behavior with product sales, providing deep insights into buying patterns.  
- The **Sales Cube** aggregates all dimensions, giving a complete view of sales performance and channel effectiveness.

---

###  Technical Highlights

- Built cubes using **Product**, **Customer**, **Time**, and **Sales Fact** dimensions.  
- Designed **measures** like Quantity and Total Price, and created a **calculated measure** for Sales Unit Price.  
- Developed a **KPI indicator** to track sales quantity performance with dynamic “faces” as status indicators.  
- Implemented **Arabic translation** for cube dimensions and measures.  
- Created **pivot tables and charts** in Excel 2007 for visual analysis.  
- Built a **perspective view** (“Channel Product Perspective”) for simplified business reporting.

---

###  Challenges & Learnings

Building these cubes required a deep understanding of how data relates across multiple dimensions.  
Designing calculated measures and KPIs while ensuring data accuracy was one of the main challenges — especially when integrating them with Excel reports.

From this experience, I learned how to:
- Optimize cube structures for performance and readability.  
- Design business-friendly KPIs and calculated measures.  
- Deliver powerful visual insights that non-technical users can easily interpret.  

This project strengthened my overall BI workflow understanding and complemented my work with **SSRS** and **SSIS**, building a complete business intelligence pipeline.
