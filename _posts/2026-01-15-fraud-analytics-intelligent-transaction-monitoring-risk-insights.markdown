**Fraud Detection Analysis Project — End-to-End Analytics & BI Platform**

The **Fraud Detection Analysis Project** was developed to tackle a critical business challenge: **identifying and monitoring fraudulent transactions in real-time while providing actionable insights for operational teams and management**.  
The system integrates **SQL-based data exploration, advanced analytics, and interactive Power BI dashboards** to create a comprehensive fraud monitoring and decision-support platform.

---

### Objective

The main objective was to design an **end-to-end fraud analytics ecosystem** capable of:

- Monitoring fraud activity and evaluating system performance
- Providing **business metrics and visual insights** for executives and operational teams
- Supporting **risk segmentation and fraud driver analysis** to reduce losses and improve detection accuracy

### System Architecture & Analytics Workflow

The project was built on a **SQL Server backend**, featuring:

- **Data Warehouse Exploration & EDA:** Using SQL queries to analyze dimensions, measures, trends, and risk segments  
- **Fraud Metrics Extraction:** Total transactions, fraud transactions, correctly detected vs missed fraud, fraud trends over time  
- **Customer & Merchant Analysis:** Ranking by risk, total fraud amounts, and transactional impact  
- **Operational & Business Dashboards:** Using **Power BI** to visualize KPIs, trends, and segmentation

The analytics layer includes three primary dashboards:

1. **Fraud Overview Dashboard (Executive)**  
   - Key metrics: Total transaction amount, total fraud amount, number of fraud transactions, detected vs missed fraud  
   - Visuals: Stacked bar for detected vs missed, pie chart for false positives/negatives, 3-month rolling trend, fraud transactions vs system alerts  

2. **Fraud Operations & Model Monitoring Dashboard**  
   - Filters: Date and account type  
   - Visuals: Confusion matrix in bar chart form, fraud detection rate over time, detection by channel, incorrectly flagged transactions over time  

3. **Fraud Drivers & Risk Segmentation Dashboard**  
   - Filters: Date range, merchant category  
   - Visuals: Fraud by customer risk segment, number of fraud transactions by account type, top merchants by total fraud amount, fraud by merchant category

---

### SQL & EDA Highlights

The project involved **deep SQL exploration** to generate insights:

- Explored all tables, columns, dimensions, and transaction dates  
- Calculated key business measures: total transactions, total fraud, detected vs missed, magnitude analysis by customer and merchant  
- Analyzed fraud trends, top customers and merchants, channel performance, and risk segments  
- Generated **confusion matrix** and rolling averages to evaluate system detection performance  

> This approach ensured that **business teams and analysts could act on accurate, data-driven insights**, while enabling IT teams to monitor and optimize fraud detection processes.
